HOW TO PLAY

Aim with your pointer or ← →. Hold and release the table, Space, or SHOOT to choose power. The longer you hold, the harder you hit.

The dotted guide shows first contact. Clear all seven colors, then the black 8-ball. You have 20 shots.

A scratch costs an extra shot and respots the cue. Sink the 8 early, or scratch on the final 8, and the rack is over.
