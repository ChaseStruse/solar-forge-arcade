# Comet Tilt gameplay

Comet Tilt is a one-player sky duel against Nova, an AI pilot. Sol, the player, and Nova each ride a rocket-tailed comet ray through a wraparound arena of floating platforms.

Use **Left Arrow** and **Right Arrow** to steer. Press **Space**, **W**, or **Up Arrow** to flap upward. Both riders retain momentum, fall under gravity, and rebound from platforms. Crossing either side of the arena wraps the rider to the opposite edge.

A clash scores when one pilot is clearly above the other. The higher pilot earns one point; a level collision knocks both riders apart without awarding a point. After a score, both riders reset to their starting sides. The first pilot to five points wins the match.

Nova continually chooses the shortest horizontal route toward Sol, including across the wraparound seam. The AI flaps when it needs the altitude advantage or when it is falling without a nearby platform. This makes its movement responsive to the player rather than scripted.
