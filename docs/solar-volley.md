# Solar Volley gameplay

Solar Volley is an 8-bit volleyball match against Nova, an AI opponent. Sol patrols the left side of the court and Nova protects the right.

Use **Left Arrow** and **Right Arrow** to move. Press **Space**, **W**, or **Up Arrow** to jump. Contact with either player sends the ball upward and carries some of the player's movement into the return, making running hits more forceful and directional.

Press jump again in midair for a second upward boost to block high shots. A third jump is unavailable until you land. Two lights above Sol show the remaining jumps. Keyboard auto-repeat does not spend the second jump; release and press again, or tap the cabinet's Jump button twice.

The net blocks low shots and rebounds balls that land on its top. A point is awarded when the ball touches the floor, and the player on the opposite side serves the next rally. The first player to five points wins the match.

Nova tracks the ball's projected horizontal position when it crosses onto the AI court. It jumps to return reachable balls and challenges shots close to the net.
