# Solar Blitz

Quick 3v3 American football: Sol attacks the right end zone; Nova attacks left.
Move with arrows or WASD, pass to the labeled receivers with X/C, dash with
Space (2.5-second cooldown), and switch defenders with Z. Catching a pass
automatically gives you control of the receiver. Defenders tackle on contact
and can intercept passes.

Each possession gets four downs. A tackle keeps the ball at the tackle spot;
an incomplete pass returns it to the snap spot. Plays expire after 14 seconds.
The other team takes possession after four downs or a touchdown. First to
three touchdowns wins, with no kicks, penalties, or extra points.
